DruidPlan

Your druid leveling companion for WoW Forever, from level 1 to 60. DruidPlan tells you what to train, which talent to take, which gear is better and where to go next, so you can spend your time playing instead of looking things up.

Pick your spec

  • Feral: Cat Form leveling with Leader of the Pack and Primal Bite.
  • Balance: Moonkin Form, Eclipse and Moonfury.
  • Restoration: healer for dungeons, with Wild Growth at 40.

The upgrade check follows your spec: attack power for Feral, spell power for Balance and healing for Restoration.

Talents

  • Talent plan for every level from 10 to 60. When you get a point, a green arrow and a glowing frame point at the next talent in WoW Forever's talent window.
  • Pick your spec in the settings window or with /dp plan. The talent plan, the upgrade check and the combat helper switch with it.
  • Every plan is checked against the live WoW Forever talent tree, so no talent is planned before its row or its prerequisite is open.

Combat helper

  • In Cat Form it calls out Clearcasting (a free attack) and 5 combo points (Rip or Ferocious Bite).
  • Reminds you of a missing Mark of the Wild or Thorns, and Rake on your target.
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /dp combat.
  • Alerts show the spell's icon next to the text, or only icons, or only text.
  • Choose the alert size and an optional alert sound (call-outs only, or every reminder) from five sounds.

Buff watcher

  • A movable row of icons that shows up when one of your buffs is missing (red frame) or running out (yellow frame, with time left). Click an icon to cast the buff. Watches Mark of the Wild and Thorns.
  • Rebuff reminder: out of combat it warns you early, by default when a buff has 3 minutes left, with a short message, a pulsing icon and a sound if you want one. Choose 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 minutes, or off.
  • Pick which buffs it watches: every buff has its own tick box under "Buffs and reminders" in the settings.
  • Turn on "Move" to drag it and Ctrl + mouse wheel to resize it. Turn it on or off in the quick menu or with /dp buffs.
  • Party marks: pick who gets Star, Circle, Diamond, Triangle and Moon in the settings (click or scroll a mark to go through your party). The addon puts the marks on them while you're in a party.
  • Buff your party: party members who are missing Mark of the Wild get their own icon with their name on it. Click it to buff them.
  • It uses the buff's own range: party members in another zone or out of range get no icon until they come closer, so you never buff yourself by accident.
  • Turn it on or off with "Buff my party too", and pick the party buffs one by one.

Spells and ranks

  • Trainer list: tells you at login and on level-up which new spells and ranks your class trainer has for you (/dp train).
  • Rank check: finds old spell ranks on your action bars, also inside macros, and new spells that aren't on a button yet (/dp ranks). Spells bound straight to a key or a controller button count too, and you can turn its chat messages off.

Gear

  • Upgrade check in item tooltips: about how much better or worse an item is than what you wear, using weights for your spec.
  • Quest reward picker: marks the best upgrade with a green + and the reward that sells for the most with a gold $. Works in the normal quest window and in DialogueUI.
  • Gear and consumable notes at the right level.

Sell, disenchant and junk

  • Bag check window (/dp de): lists your junk, the gear to sell to a vendor, the gear to disenchant and the gear worth more on the auction house, with what each is worth (AH prices from Auctionator if you have it).
  • Sell from the window: with a vendor open, click a row to sell it, or one button sells your junk and vendor items, 12 at a time so you can still buy them back.
  • Disenchant from the window if you have Enchanting: click a row to disenchant it. Shift-click sells anything to the vendor anyway.
  • Right-click an item to keep it: it's never sold or disenchanted from the window.
  • Opens by itself when you talk to a vendor and have something to sell. You can also open it with the small disenchant icon above your open bags (also with Baganator and other bag addons) or by middle-clicking the minimap icon.
  • Sell, disenchant and AH advice also shows in item tooltips.

Dungeons

  • Dungeon guide: pick a dungeon to see its quests, where to pick them up and the druid loot from its bosses.
  • Alliance and Horde quests and quest givers, Ragefire Chasm and the new WoW Forever dungeons: Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands, City of Dalaran, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace.
  • Waypoint arrow to the quest giver or the dungeon entrance.
  • Can open by itself when you enter a dungeon.

Professions

  • Pick your own two professions in the settings window, from all nine: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Herbalism, Leatherworking, Mining, Skinning and Tailoring. If you don't pick, the addon uses the professions you have learned, or Alchemy and Herbalism.
  • Crafting assistant for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring: what to craft for every skill bracket and the materials you need, following routes made for WoW Forever.
  • Gathering help for Herbalism, Mining and Skinning: which herbs, ore and leather you can gather now and the best zones for your skill.
  • Alliance and Horde: trainers, vendors and zones for your own faction, and the trainer for your next profession rank shown right in the window.
  • Gear and consumable notes for your professions, and a reminder when it's time to train the next profession rank.
  • Cooking, First Aid and Fishing too, without using a profession slot: what to cook or make for every skill range and where to learn or buy the recipe, where to fish and with which lures, and where the Expert books and Artisan quests are. Tick the ones you want in the settings.

And more

  • XP tracker with XP per hour and time to level.
  • Drink alert: out of combat with low mana, a big "Drink!" with an icon in the middle of the screen (casters and hybrids). It works even though WoW Forever hides your mana from addons.
  • Walk on Air timer for Skyborne: a big countdown with a bar so you land before you fall.
  • Gold goal, for example /dp goal 100 mount.
  • Zone tips when you enter a zone.
  • Minimap icon: left-click for a quick menu (spec and on/off switches, sorted into groups), right-click for the full settings window with your class logo, where everything is sorted into clear groups, and middle-click for the bag check.
  • Movable, lockable windows and layout profiles shared by all your characters.
  • English and Swedish.
  • Easter eggs with friends who also run a Plan addon: try /kiss, /dance, /cheer, /hug, /wave, /drink, /chicken, /train, /sleep, /laugh, /salute, /cry and /flex on them. /dance at each other for a dance party, and everyone gets fireworks when one of you levels up. You can turn them off.

Getting started

Type /dp to open the plan window, /dp config for settings and /dp help for all commands. You can also right-click the minimap icon.

DruidPlan only runs on druid characters, so you can keep all the Plan addons installed: MagePlan, ShamanPlan, DruidPlan, WarriorPlan, PaladinPlan, WarlockPlan, PriestPlan, HunterPlan and RoguePlan.
