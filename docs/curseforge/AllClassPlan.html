AllClassPlan

Your leveling companion for every class in WoW Forever, from level 1 to 60, in one install. AllClassPlan tells you what to train, which talent to take, which gear is better and where to go next, so you can spend your time playing instead of looking things up. It is all nine Plan addons (MagePlan, ShamanPlan, DruidPlan, WarriorPlan, PaladinPlan, WarlockPlan, PriestPlan, HunterPlan and RoguePlan) in one: log in on any character and you get the right one for its class.

Coming from MagePlan, PaladinPlan or another Plan addon?

  • Your settings come along: install AllClassPlan and keep your old Plan addon installed. The first time each character logs in, its plan, windows, text sizes, profiles and language are copied over.
  • No doubles: the old addon steps aside by itself on characters where AllClassPlan runs. Once all your characters have logged in once, you can remove the old ones.
  • Your commands still work: /mp, /sp, /dp, /war, /pal, /wl, /pp, /hp and /rp all work on every character, same as /acp.

Every class, its own plan and tools

Mage (also /mp): Frost leveling, Frost solo questing, Frost AoE grinding, Fire leveling, Arcane.

  • Shatter indicator: tells you when your target is frozen or Fingers of Frost procs and Ice Lance hits hard, and when Ice Barrier is missing.
  • Polymorph alerts: warns 5 seconds before your sheep breaks, and when it breaks early.
  • Conjured food, water and mana gem tracker that warns when you are running low.
  • Group bar in a party: one click trades a stack of water to mana users or food to warriors and rogues, the best rank their level can use. It trades your target, or the next party member in range who hasn't had any, and party members who open a trade with you get it too.
  • Merchant's Favor goals for Tailoring and Enchanting when you have them.

Shaman (also /sp): Enhancement, Elemental, Restoration.

  • Calls out Maelstrom Weapon at 5 stacks, so you never miss an instant Lightning Bolt.
  • Calls out Stormstrike the moment it's ready. With Improved Stormstrike every dodge and parry resets it, so you never miss a free Stormstrike.
  • Reminds you of a missing Lightning Shield, weapon imbue or Flame Shock.
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /acp combat.

Druid (also /dp): Feral, Balance, Restoration.

  • In Cat Form it calls out Clearcasting (a free attack) and 5 combo points (Rip or Ferocious Bite).
  • Reminds you of a missing Mark of the Wild or Thorns, and Rake on your target.
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /acp combat.

Warrior (also /war): Arms, Fury, Protection.

  • Calls out Overpower (Arms and Fury) and Revenge (Protection) when they light up.
  • Calls out Execute when your target drops below 20% and Victory Rush after a kill.
  • Reminds you of a missing Battle Shout, Rend on your target (Arms) or Demoralizing Shout (Protection).
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /acp combat.

Paladin (also /pal): Protection, Retribution, Holy.

  • Calls out Hammer of Wrath when your target drops below 20%, and Exorcism against undead and demons.
  • Reminds you of a missing aura (pick which one in the settings, or Auto), Righteous Fury (Protection), seal or blessing, and when Holy Shield is ready.
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /acp combat.

Warlock (also /wl): Affliction, Demonology, Destruction.

  • Group bar in a party: one click trades your best healthstone to your target, or to the next party member in range who hasn't had one, and party members who open a trade with you get one too.
  • Calls out Shadow Trance (an instant Shadow Bolt from Nightfall), Shadowburn when your target is low, Decimation (a fast Soul Fire) and Conflagrate.
  • Reminds you of a missing Corruption, Bane of Agony, Siphon Life or Immolate on your target.
  • Tells you to Life Tap when your mana is low and your health is high, to summon your demon, and when you are out of Soul Shards.
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /acp combat.

Priest (also /pp): Shadow, Holy, Discipline.

  • Calls out Mind Blast (Shadow) and Penance (Discipline) when they are ready.
  • Reminds you of a missing Shadow Word: Pain or Vampiric Embrace on your target, and a missing Inner Fire.
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /acp combat.

Hunter (also /hp): Beast Mastery, Marksmanship, Survival.

  • Ammo alert: counts the arrows or bullets in your bags and quiver and warns you out of combat, in the middle of the screen, before you run dry (below 200 by default, /acp ammo 300 to change it).
  • Gear notes tell you when better arrows and bullets come out (levels 10, 25 and 40).
  • Pet reminders: tells you when your pet is missing or dead, and when it's low and needs Mend Pet.
  • Reminds you of a missing Hunter's Mark or Serpent Sting on your target.
  • Survival: calls out Mongoose Bite and Counterattack when they light up.
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /acp combat.

Rogue (also /rp): Combat, Assassination, Subtlety.

  • Poisons in the buff watcher: an icon for each weapon without poison. Click it and the best poison in your bags goes on that weapon (Instant on the main hand, Deadly on the off-hand). It warns 5 minutes before a poison runs out.
  • Calls out 5 combo points (time for Eviscerate or Slice and Dice) and Riposte for Combat.
  • Reminds you when Slice and Dice is down, and of Rupture on your target for Assassination.
  • Tells you about the level 20 poison quest, so you don't miss it.
  • Movable and resizable, and you can turn it off with /acp combat.

The upgrade check follows your spec, with the stats that matter for it.

Talents

  • Talent plan for every level from 10 to 60. When you get a point, a green arrow and a glowing frame point at the next talent in WoW Forever's talent window.
  • Your talent window shows the whole plan: the next talent gets the green arrow and frame, every talent still left in your plan gets a yellow frame, and a small green tick in the corner marks the ones you have finished. It also works in replacement UIs like Daylight UI.
  • Pick your spec in the settings window or with /acp plan. The talent plan, the upgrade check and the combat helper switch with it.
  • Every plan is checked against the live WoW Forever talent tree, so no talent is planned before its row or its prerequisite is open.
  • Your own build: make it in Icy Veins' WoW Forever talent calculator (or on wow-forever-forge.com), copy the address of your build and paste it with "Import talents" in the settings (or /acp import). These links keep the order you clicked the talents in, so it becomes your leveling plan from level 10 exactly as you built it. Links from Wowhead's calculator and talentsforever.com work too (they fill your main tree first).
  • Put your points somewhere else? Right-click "Next talent" (or /acp skip) to skip a talent in the plan.

Buff watcher

  • A movable row of icons that shows up when one of your buffs is missing (red frame) or running out (yellow frame, with time left). Click an icon to cast the buff. Watches your class buffs (Arcane Intellect, Fortitude, Mark of the Wild, blessings, aspects, poisons, Demon Armor, shields...).
  • Rebuff reminder: out of combat it warns you early, by default when a buff has 3 minutes left, with a short message, a pulsing icon and a sound if you want one. Choose 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 minutes, or off.
  • Pick which buffs it watches: every buff has its own tick box under "Buffs and reminders" in the settings.
  • Turn on "Move" to drag it and Ctrl + mouse wheel to resize it. Turn it on or off in the quick menu or with /acp buffs.
  • Party marks: pick who gets Star, Circle, Diamond, Triangle and Moon in the settings. In a party a small mark button shows: one click puts the marks on, also in combat after the tank's marking took them away.
  • Buff your party: party members who are missing your party buff get their own icon with their name on it. Click it to buff them.
  • It uses the buff's own range: party members in another zone or out of range get no icon until they come closer, so you never buff yourself by accident.
  • Turn it on or off with "Buff my party too", and pick the party buffs one by one.

Spells and ranks

  • Trainer list: tells you at login and on level-up which new spells and ranks your class trainer has for you (/acp train).
  • Rank check: finds old spell ranks on your action bars, also inside macros, and new spells that aren't on a button yet (/acp ranks). Spells bound straight to a key or a controller button count too, in the settings you choose whether the standard bars (buttons 1-180), the controller's button sets (181-360) or both are checked, and you can turn its chat messages off.

Gear

  • Upgrade check in item tooltips: about how much better or worse an item is than what you wear, using weights for your spec.
  • Quest reward picker: marks the best upgrade with a green + and the reward that sells for the most with a gold $. Works in the normal quest window and in DialogueUI.
  • Gear and consumable notes at the right level.

Sell, disenchant and junk

  • Bag check window (/acp de): lists your junk, the gear to sell to a vendor, the gear to disenchant and the gear worth more on the auction house, with what each is worth (AH prices from Auctionator if you have it).
  • Sell from the window: with a vendor open, click a row to sell it, or one button sells your junk and vendor items, 12 at a time so you can still buy them back.
  • Disenchant from the window if you have Enchanting: click a row to disenchant it. Shift-click sells anything to the vendor anyway.
  • Right-click an item to keep it: it's never sold or disenchanted from the window.
  • Opens by itself when you talk to a vendor and have something to sell. You can also open it with the small disenchant icon above your open bags (also with Baganator and other bag addons) or by middle-clicking the minimap icon.
  • Sell, disenchant and AH advice also shows in item tooltips.

Dungeons

  • Dungeon guide: pick a dungeon to see its quests, where to pick them up and the loot for your class from its bosses.
  • Alliance and Horde quests and quest givers, Ragefire Chasm and the new WoW Forever dungeons: Hall of Thanes, Ruins of Lordaeron, Excavation Site: Wetlands, City of Dalaran, The Drowned City, Krol'dok Stronghold, Alcaz Prison, Blackmaw Hold and Shaper's Terrace.
  • Waypoint arrow to the quest giver or the dungeon entrance.
  • Can open by itself when you enter a dungeon.

Professions

  • Pick your own two professions in the settings window, from all nine: Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Herbalism, Leatherworking, Mining, Skinning and Tailoring. If you don't pick, the addon uses the professions you have learned, or a good pair for your class.
  • Crafting assistant for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking and Tailoring: what to craft for every skill bracket and the materials you need, following routes made for WoW Forever.
  • Gathering help for Herbalism, Mining and Skinning: which herbs, ore and leather you can gather now and the best zones for your skill.
  • Alliance and Horde: trainers, vendors and zones for your own faction, and the trainer for your next profession rank shown right in the window.
  • Gear and consumable notes for your professions, and a reminder when it's time to train the next profession rank.
  • Cooking, First Aid and Fishing too, without using a profession slot: what to cook or make for every skill range and where to learn or buy the recipe, where to fish and with which lures, and where the Expert books and Artisan quests are. Tick the ones you want in the settings.

And more

  • XP tracker with XP per hour and time to level.
  • Drink alert: out of combat with low mana, a big "Drink!" with an icon in the middle of the screen (casters and hybrids). It works even though WoW Forever hides your mana from addons.
  • Walk on Air timer for Skyborne: a big countdown with a bar so you land before you fall.
  • Gold goal, for example /acp goal 100 mount.
  • Zone tips when you enter a zone.
  • Minimap icon: left-click for a quick menu (spec and on/off switches, sorted into groups), right-click for the full settings window where everything is sorted into clear groups, and middle-click for the bag check.
  • Movable, lockable windows and layout profiles shared by all your characters.
  • Make it fit you: drag a window's corner to resize it, Ctrl + mouse wheel to scale it, and right-click the corner to give that window its own text size (80% to 160%). The Text size setting (or /acp font 120) sets it for all of them. Want taller windows? Turn on "Free window height" (or /acp height) to drag the corner in height too, and scroll inside with the mouse wheel.
  • English and Swedish.
  • Easter eggs with friends who also run a Plan addon: try /kiss, /dance, /cheer, /hug, /wave, /drink, /chicken, /train, /sleep, /laugh, /salute, /cry, /flex, /fart, /sing, /gasp, /moo, /thank, /charge, /violin, /bow, /slap and /eat on them. /dance or /hug at each other for a dance party or a group hug, three of you who /drink in a party get a round, and everyone gets fireworks when one of you levels up (level 60 gets the grand finale). Emote with nobody targeted and your whole party sees it. Try to loot with full bags and your bag throws a tantrum. You can turn them off.

Getting started

Type /acp to open the plan window, /acp config for settings and /acp help for all commands. You can also right-click the minimap icon. Only the part for your character's class is loaded, so AllClassPlan stays light.
