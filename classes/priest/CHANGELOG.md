# PriestPlan 1.0.0-beta71

- **AllClassPlan is out!** All nine Plan addons now come in one install: AllClassPlan. Once per character you'll see a notice with the link. Your settings come along when you install it. This is the last update of this addon.

- **New languages: Deutsch, Русский, Français, Español and Português (BR).** Every menu text, message and tip is translated. Until you pick a language yourself, the addon uses your game's language when it has it (otherwise English). Change it in the menu (Language) or with `/pp lang` (or `/pp lang de`, `ru`, `fr`, `es`, `pt`, `sv`, `en`).

- **Getting ready for AllClassPlan** (all Plan addons in one install, coming soon): when AllClassPlan is installed and turned on, PriestPlan steps aside on that character and AllClassPlan takes over with your settings. Without AllClassPlan nothing changes.

- **New easter egg:** when somebody in your group rolls Need on an item, a die tumbles in with "NEED!" and a line about the greedy one (at most every 15 seconds, only you see it). Preview with `/pp egg need`.

- **Buff bar:** a buff that someone else's stronger buff blocks (Mark of the Wild on a player with Might of Stormwind, "a more powerful spell is already active") no longer shows an icon. If the game refuses a click for that reason, the icon is hidden for that player for 10 minutes.

- **Easter eggs:** the full-bags egg now has three versions (the bag bursts, the bag burps, or the bag runs away), picked at random. Preview with `/pp egg full`.

- **Free window height:** new setting "Free window height (scroll inside)" (also `/pp height`). With it on, drag the corner of the plan or crafting window to any height and scroll inside with the mouse wheel. Off, the height follows the content as before.
- **New easter egg:** try to loot with full bags and your bag bursts, with junk flying everywhere (only you see it, at most every two minutes).
- **Easter eggs:** the one who starts a party emote no longer sees it twice (your own message came back to you).
